ASSOCIATION OF MUTUAL FUNDS IN INDIA (AMFI) - CODE OF CONDUCT

PART A: CODE OF CONDUCT FOR INTERMEDIARIES OF MUTUAL FUNDS

1. Integrity
Intermediaries shall maintain high standards of integrity and fairness in all their dealings with investors and the mutual fund.

2. Know Your Distributor (KYD)
All intermediaries are required to comply with KYD norms. AMFI Registration Number (ARN) is mandatory for distributing mutual fund products.

3. Suitability
Intermediaries shall ensure that the mutual fund schemes recommended are suitable for the investor based on:
(a) the investor's risk appetite
(b) the investor's investment horizon
(c) the investor's financial situation
(d) the investor's investment objectives

4. Fair Dealing
Intermediaries shall not:
(a) make false or misleading statements about the performance of any scheme
(b) guarantee or assure any return to the investor
(c) use any unfair or deceptive practice in dealing with investors
(d) rebate commissions directly or indirectly to investors

5. Disclosure
Intermediaries shall disclose to the investor:
(a) all commissions received from the mutual fund
(b) any conflict of interest
(c) the complete terms and conditions of the investment

PART B: CATEGORIZATION AND RATIONALIZATION OF MUTUAL FUND SCHEMES

As per SEBI circular dated October 6, 2017, all mutual fund schemes must be classified under the following categories:
1. Equity Schemes (Large Cap, Mid Cap, Small Cap, Multi Cap, etc.)
2. Debt Schemes (Overnight, Liquid, Ultra Short Duration, etc.)
3. Hybrid Schemes (Conservative Hybrid, Balanced Advantage, etc.)
4. Solution Oriented Schemes (Retirement Fund, Children's Fund)
5. Other Schemes (Index Funds, ETFs, Fund of Funds)
