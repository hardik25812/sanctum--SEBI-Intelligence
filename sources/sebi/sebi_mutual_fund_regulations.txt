SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA (MUTUAL FUNDS) REGULATIONS, 1996

CHAPTER V — RESTRICTIONS ON INVESTMENTS

Regulation 44: Investment Restrictions
(1) No mutual fund under all its schemes should own more than ten per cent of any company's paid up capital carrying voting rights.
(2) The transfers of investments from one scheme to another scheme of the same mutual fund shall be allowed only if such transfers are done at the prevailing market price for quoted instruments on spot basis.
(3) A scheme shall not invest more than 10% of its NAV in the equity shares or equity related instruments of any company. Provided that, the limit of 10% shall not be applicable for investments in index fund or sector or industry specific scheme.
(4) A mutual fund scheme shall not invest more than 5% of its NAV in the unlisted equity shares or equity related instruments in case of open-ended schemes.

Regulation 44A: Investment in debt instruments
(1) A mutual fund scheme shall not invest more than 10% of its NAV in debt instruments comprising money market instruments and non-money market instruments issued by a single issuer which are rated not below investment grade by a credit rating agency.
(2) The limit in sub-regulation (1) may be extended to 12% of the NAV of the scheme with the prior approval of the Board of Trustees.

CHAPTER VI — GENERAL OBLIGATIONS

Regulation 48: Obligation of mutual fund
(1) Every mutual fund shall compute and carry out valuation of its investments in its portfolio and publish the same in accordance with the regulations.
(2) The Net Asset Value of every scheme shall be calculated and published at least in two daily newspapers at intervals not exceeding one week.
