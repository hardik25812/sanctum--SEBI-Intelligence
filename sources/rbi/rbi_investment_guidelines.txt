RESERVE BANK OF INDIA - KEY GUIDELINES FOR RETAIL INVESTORS

1. Fixed Deposit Insurance
Deposits with scheduled commercial banks are insured by the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC) up to ₹5,00,000 per depositor per bank.

2. Priority Sector Lending
As per RBI guidelines, banks are required to lend a specified percentage of their adjusted net bank credit to priority sectors including agriculture, MSMEs, education, housing, and social infrastructure.

3. Digital Payment Guidelines
RBI's guidelines on digital payments ensure:
(a) Two-factor authentication for all electronic transactions
(b) Customer liability limits in case of unauthorized transactions
(c) Turn-around time for resolution of failed transactions

4. External Commercial Borrowings (ECB)
Companies may raise ECB under the automatic route within certain limits:
(a) Up to USD 750 million per financial year under Track I
(b) Minimum average maturity period requirements apply

5. NRI Investment Framework
Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) may invest in India through:
(a) Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) — up to 5% of paid-up capital of an Indian company
(b) Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) — as per sectoral caps
(c) NRE/NRO deposit accounts with repatriation facilities

6. Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme
RBI issues Sovereign Gold Bonds on behalf of the Government of India:
(a) Tenor: 8 years with exit option from 5th year
(b) Annual interest rate: 2.50% on the nominal value
(c) Tax: Capital gains tax exemption on redemption
